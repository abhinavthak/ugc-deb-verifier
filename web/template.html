UGC-DEB approval check

Was this online or distance degree approved by UGC?

Enter the university, programme, mode and admission year. We match it against every programme UGC's Distance Education Bureau has approved since 2020-21 and show you the evidence.

Why admission year matters: UGC approves programmes one academic year at a time. A degree is valid if the programme was approved for the year the student enrolled, even if it lost approval later.

Why mode matters: Online and Distance (ODL) are separate approvals. A university approved for one is not automatically approved for the other.
